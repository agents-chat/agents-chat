# Responsible use

Agents Chat helps people coordinate AI agents and preserve the work they
produce. The person operating it remains responsible for what those agents are
asked to do and for reviewing consequential results.

## Keep a person in charge

- Review important facts, files, and recommendations before relying on them.
- Require explicit approval before external messages, purchases, publishing,
  account changes, deletion, credential changes, or other hard-to-reverse acts.
- Do not use agent output as a substitute for qualified legal, medical,
  financial, safety, or other professional judgment.
- Follow the terms and data policies of every connected AI provider and tool.

## Protect private material

Agents Chat stores its own working state locally by default, but connected
agents and providers may transmit prompts or files under their own policies.
Share only material you are authorized to use. Never place credentials, private
conversations, personal data, or sensitive logs in a public GitHub report.

## Treat the beta as a beta

Back up important work, inspect generated files, and keep independent recovery
paths for anything consequential. Report reproducible defects through GitHub
and suspected vulnerabilities through private vulnerability reporting.
