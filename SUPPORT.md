# Support

Agents Chat is a personal open-source beta maintained by Eric Miller, not a
commercial support service. I review GitHub regularly, but responses and fixes
are not guaranteed.

## Where to ask

- Use [Discussions](https://github.com/agents-chat/agents-chat/discussions) for
  installation help, questions, ideas, and examples of what you built.
- Use a structured [Issue](https://github.com/agents-chat/agents-chat/issues/new/choose)
  for a reproducible bug or documentation problem.
- Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities. Never post
  credentials, private chats, personal data, or unredacted logs publicly.

## What receives priority

Security, data-loss risks, and installation-blocking problems come first. I aim
to triage reproducible bug reports within seven days and acknowledge private
security reports within three business days, without guaranteeing a particular
response or fix time.

Reports that need more information may be labeled `needs-info` and closed after
21 days without a response. They can be reopened when the missing evidence is
available. Feature votes show interest; they are not a delivery promise.
