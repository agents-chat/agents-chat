# Support

Agents Chat is a personal open-source beta, not a commercial support service. I
review GitHub regularly, but responses and fixes are not guaranteed.

## Where to ask

- Open a structured [question](https://github.com/agents-chat/agents-chat/issues/new?template=question.yml)
  for installation help, connection help, and how-to questions.
- Open a structured [feature request](https://github.com/agents-chat/agents-chat/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
  for an idea or workflow you would like to see.
- Use a structured [Issue](https://github.com/agents-chat/agents-chat/issues/new/choose)
  for a reproducible bug or documentation problem.
- Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for suspected vulnerabilities. Never post
  credentials, private chats, personal data, or unredacted logs publicly.

## What receives priority

Security, data-loss risks, and installation-blocking problems come first. I aim
to triage reproducible bug reports within seven days and acknowledge private
security reports within three business days, without guaranteeing a particular
response or fix time.

Reports that need more information may be closed after 21 days without a
response. They can be reopened when the missing evidence is available. Feature
votes show interest; they are not a delivery promise.
